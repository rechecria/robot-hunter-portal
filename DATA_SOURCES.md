### Pain Points (Real Data from Panorama)
1. **Labor Shortage** - 67% of farmers cite as #1 or #2 challenge
   - California: $16.3B in 850M hours @ $20/hour (2023)
   - Japan: Severe agricultural population aging
   
2. **Chemical Dependence** - Herbicides, pesticides, fungicides
   - Ecorobotix: 95% reduction with plant-by-plant AI
   - Verdant: 96% reduction with 5mm micro-spray
   - Carbon Robotics: 100% elimination (laser)
   - Aigen/FarmDroid: 100% elimination (mechanical)

3. **Soil Compaction** - €1.2B/year in EU losses
   - Fendt Xaver: 150 kg vs 10,000+ kg tractor
   - FarmDroid: 900 kg, solar-powered
   - AgXeed: <30 kPa soil pressure with tracks

4. **Limited Operating Windows** - 8-12h vs 24h autonomy
   - AgXeed: 24h diesel-electric operation
   - Robotti: 60h energy autonomy
   - Fendt Xaver: 6-robot swarm, 3 ha/h

5. **Lack of Precision Data** - Loss from guesswork
   - Solinftec: IoT + IA ALICE platform
   - Real-time sensors and integration
